================================================================================
          THE INFECTED HOUR — ONLINE CO-OP PRESENTATION GUIDE
================================================================================

PROBLEM:
Our project presentation will be conducted ONLINE (Google Meet / Zoom / Discord).
Our game's multiplayer is built on LAN (KryoNet TCP 54555 / UDP 54777).

BELOW ARE THE 3 WAYS TO DEMONSTRATE CO-OP ONLINE TO OUR TEACHER.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OPTION 1: SPLIT-SCREEN SIDE-BY-SIDE ON 1 PC (⭐ STRONGLY RECOMMENDED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* BEST FOR: 100% stability, zero Wi-Fi/Internet lag, maximum visual impact.
* CONCEPT: The presenter runs 1 Host and 1 Client on the same laptop/PC,
           places them side-by-side, and shares their full screen on Meet/Zoom.

WHY TEACHERS LOVE THIS:
- The teacher doesn't have to switch between two students' screens or wait.
- Both player perspectives are visible simultaneously on 1 screen.
- When Player 1 moves/attacks on the left window, Player 2's screen on the
  right immediately updates in real-time.
- Demonstrates real-time packet sync (TCP reliable events + UDP 20Hz state
  snapshots) without any risk of home internet disconnects during the viva!

STEP-BY-STEP COMMANDS:
1. Open Terminal 1 (Spring Boot Backend):
   .\gradlew.bat :backend:bootRun

2. Open Terminal 2 (Player 1 — Host / Elric):
   .\gradlew.bat :lwjgl3:run
   (Alternatively: run .\gradlew.bat :fx-launcher:run and click Host Game)

3. Open Terminal 3 (Player 2 — Client / Jane):
   .\gradlew.bat :lwjgl3:run --args="join"
   (This connects instantly to localhost 127.0.0.1!)

4. Arrange Windows on Screen:
   - Press [F11] on both game windows to switch to Windowed mode (1280x720).
   - Snap Player 1 window to Left half:   Press [Win] + [Left Arrow]
   - Snap Player 2 window to Right half:  Press [Win] + [Right Arrow]

5. In Google Meet / Zoom:
   - Click "Share Screen" -> Choose "Entire Screen".
   - You can control Player 1 with WASD + Mouse, and show Player 2 reacting,
     or alternate between windows to demonstrate co-op interactions.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OPTION 2: VIRTUAL LAN VIA RADMIN VPN (PLAYING FROM 2 DIFFERENT HOUSES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* BEST FOR: If two teammates are at their own homes and both want to play live
  on their own keyboards simultaneously during the presentation.

SETUP TIME: ~3 minutes (Free, no account needed).

STEP 1: INSTALLATION
- Both teammates download and install Radmin VPN:
  Website: https://www.radmin-vpn.com/

STEP 2: CREATE VIRTUAL NETWORK
- Teammate 1 (Host):
  1. Open Radmin VPN -> Click "Create Network".
  2. Choose a Network Name (e.g., "InfectedHourDemo2026") and a password.
  3. Note down your Radmin Virtual IP address (it looks like: 26.xxx.xxx.xxx).
  4. Start the backend:
     .\gradlew.bat :backend:bootRun
  5. Start the Host Launcher:
     .\gradlew.bat :fx-launcher:run -> Host Lobby -> Start Game.

STEP 3: CLIENT CONNECTS
- Teammate 2 (Client at their home):
  1. Open Radmin VPN -> Click "Join Network".
  2. Enter the same Network Name and Password.
  3. Start the launcher:
     .\gradlew.bat :fx-launcher:run
  4. Go to "Co-op Mode" -> "Join a Match".
  5. In the box "...or connect directly by IP", type the Host's Radmin IP:
     Example: 26.152.88.42
  6. Click "Join".

STEP 4: PRESENTING ON CALL
- In Google Meet / Zoom, the Host shares their screen.
- Both teammates speak on their mics and coordinate gameplay live.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OPTION 3: PRE-RECORDED VIDEO BACKUP (SAFETY NET — MUST PREPARE!)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* WHY: Live network demos over video calls can suffer from lag, mic feedback,
  or ISP hiccups. Having a clean 1-2 minute video ensures 0% stress.

WHAT TO RECORD:
- 60 to 90 seconds showing:
  1. Lobby joining / character selection (Elric + Jane).
  2. Both characters moving and clearing infected together.
  3. Synchronized objective completion / puzzle interaction.
  4. Downed state and reviving mechanic.

HOW TO RECORD:
- Use Windows Game Bar ([Win] + [G]) or OBS Studio.
- Save the video as: "Infected_Hour_Coop_Demo.mp4" on your Desktop.
- You can also embed the clip into your PowerPoint presentation slide.

WHAT TO SAY IF INTERNET GLITCHES:
"Sir, we have the live system running, and we have also attached a 1080p capture
demonstrating the 2-player KryoNet packet synchronization in case screen-share
bandwidth stutters."
