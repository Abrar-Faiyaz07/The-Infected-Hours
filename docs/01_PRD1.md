## 1. Vision

The Infected Hour is a 2D top-down cooperative action game where two players work together to contain a spreading epidemic in a remote village and destroy its source — the **Virus Heart**. Unlike combat-only epidemic games, gameplay is built around real public health procedures: isolation, sanitation, sample collection, medicine delivery, and rescue. Aligned with **UN SDG 3: Good Health and Well-Being**.

**Core innovation:** The only 2D co-op game combining action gameplay with genuine public health education, wrapped in an emotional narrative of redemption.

## 2. Target Users

- Primary: Students and casual gamers (ages 13+) who enjoy co-op pixel games (Stardew Valley, Left 4 Dead audiences)
- Secondary: Educators looking for engaging public health teaching tools
- Evaluators: Course instructors assessing technical completeness

## 3. Platform & Play Model

- Desktop (Windows/Linux/macOS), Java 21+
- **Networked local co-op over LAN/Wi-Fi**: Laptop A = Host + Player 1 (Elric), Laptop B = Client + Player 2 (Jane). Both laptops must be on the same network.
- Single-player fallback: host can start a match solo for testing/demo (Player 2 slot empty; missions balance down).

## 4. Characters

### Elric — Field Medic (Player 1, Host)
- Heals self and partner (medkit ability, cooldown-based)
- Deploys cure samples (key boss mechanic)
- Passive: 30% contamination resistance (slower contamination meter gain in toxic zones)
- Slower movement speed than Jane

### Jane — Local Scout (Player 2, Client)
- Faster movement; can dash (cooldown-based)
- Rescues villagers faster (reduced interaction time)
- Activates sanitation points faster
- Passive: mini-map reveals larger radius ("knows the terrain")

## 5. Core Gameplay Loop

1. Read mission briefing (level objectives shown on start)
2. Explore village map; avoid expanding contamination clouds
3. Complete public-health objectives (below) while fighting/avoiding infected enemies
4. Keep global **Contamination Meter** below 100% — at 100% the village is lost (level failed)
5. Complete all objectives → level cleared → **story panel** reveals character backstory → next level

## 6. Mission Types (Objectives)

| Objective | Description | Primary character |
|---|---|---|
| Isolate Infected Zones | Carry barricade parts and place barricades at zone entrances to stop spread | Either |
| Collect Samples | Interact with sample nodes inside contaminated zones; deliver to lab station | Elric |
| Deliver Medicine | Pick up medicine crates and carry to quarantined villager houses | Either |
| Rescue Villagers | Escort/carry NPC villagers to safe zone before their infection timer expires | Jane |
| Activate Sanitation | Hold-interact at sanitation stations to cleanse an area (creates safe zone) | Jane |
| Avoid Contamination Clouds | Dynamic toxic zones expand over time; standing inside raises personal contamination | Both |

## 7. Levels

| Level | Name | Teaches / Combines | Fail condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Village Outskirts | Movement, interaction, barricades, sample collection, first contamination cloud | Contamination 100% |
| 2 | Market District | Medicine delivery + villager rescue + sanitation + enemies (all remaining mechanics) | Contamination 100% or all villagers lost |
| 3 (Boss) | The Virus Heart | All mechanics + 3-phase cooperative boss fight | Contamination 100% |

Level 1 teaches basics, Level 2 combines everything under pressure, Level 3 is the boss. (The 4-level version with a separate "Old Hospital" level is **[STRETCH]**.)

## 8. Boss Fight — The Virus Heart (Level 3)

A living epidemic at the village core, protected by mutated cell enemies, spreading toxic zones, and expanding infection waves.

- **Phase 1 — Shield Phase:** Elric collects and places cure samples at 3 injection points to weaken the shield; Jane defends him from mutation swarms and protects trapped villagers.
- **Phase 2 — Exposure Phase:** Shield drops for a timed window; both players attack the exposed core together. Core re-shields after the window; return to Phase 1 (shield weaker each cycle).
- **Phase 3 — Core Destruction:** At low core HP, final coordinated assault; arena floods with infection waves; both players must deal simultaneous damage (Elric's cure charge + Jane's strike) to finish.
- **Failure:** arena contamination reaches 100% → village lost.
- **Victory:** core destroyed → ending story panels → credits.

## 9. Narrative

Story delivered through **visual story panels** (illustrated stills + text) after each level. The village is Elric's childhood home; his family was affected by an earlier outbreak. Jane grew up in these streets. Across 3 levels the mission transforms into a personal journey of redemption. 4 panel sequences total: intro, after L1 (Elric's family flashback), after L2 (Jane's streets + the earlier outbreak truth), ending (redemption).

## 10. Player Systems

- **Health bar** (per player) — damaged by enemies; healed by Elric/medkits. 0 HP = downed; partner can revive within 30s, else level restart.
- **Personal contamination bar** — rises in toxic zones; at 100% player takes continuous HP damage until cleansed at a sanitation point.
- **Global contamination meter** — the level's doom clock; rises over time and when zones expand; lowered by barricades/sanitation objectives.
- **Inventory** (4 slots per player) — samples, medicine, barricade parts, medkits, quest items.
- **Mini-map** — objectives, contamination zones, villagers, partner position.

## 11. Persistence & Backend (summary — see Backend Schema doc)

- Player profiles with username/password, character stats, cosmetic-free progression
- Save/level progress synced to Spring Boot backend so **both laptops receive the save** after a multiplayer session
- Leaderboards: fastest level clears, lowest contamination %, boss completion time
- Match history: who played, roles, result, duration, per-player stats

## 12. Audio

- Atmospheric looping music per level (free-license packs)
- SFX: footsteps, interaction, barricade place, sanitation hiss, enemy hits, heartbeat when contamination > 75%, boss roars

## 13. Success Criteria (Definition of Done — 6-day version)

1. Two laptops on the same Wi-Fi can host/join and complete all 3 levels co-op with playable responsiveness on LAN
2. All 6 mission types implemented across the levels
3. 3-phase boss fight functional and requiring both players
4. HUD: health, contamination bars, global meter, inventory hotbar, mini-map (simple version)
5. 4 story panel sequences displayed at correct points
6. Spring Boot backend live: login, save sync to both machines, leaderboard, match history
7. Stable 60 FPS on mid-range laptops; no crash-to-desktop across a full playthrough
