Task A. Download Speed
Time Limit: 2 seconds|Memory Limit: 512 MB
Consider a network ofncomputers andmconnections. Each connection allows one computer to send data to
another at a specific speed. If there are multiple connections between the same two computers, their speeds add
up. Kotivalo wants to download data from a server to his computer. What is the maximum total speed he can
achieve?
Input Format
The first input line has two integersnandm: the number of computers and connections. The computers are
numbered 1,2, . . . , n. Computer 1 is the server and computernis Kotivalo’s computer. After this, there arem
lines describing the connections. Each line has three integersa,b, andc: computeracan send data to computer
bat speedc.
Output Format
Print one integer: the maximum speed Kotivalo can download data.
Constraints

1≤m≤1000
1≤a, b≤n
1≤c≤10 9

Task B. Checkpoint Shutdown
Time Limit: 2 seconds|Memory Limit: 512 MB
There arencrossings andmone-way streets in a city. The Reveal Team wants to travel from crossing 1 to crossing
n. The police want to prevent this by shutting down crossings. Shutting down a crossing removes it, along with
every street entering or leaving it. Crossings 1 andncannot be shut down. Find the minimum number of crossings
that must be shut down so that no route from crossing 1 to crossingnremains.
Input Format
The first input line has two integersnandm: the number of crossings and streets. The crossings are numbered
1,2, . . . , n. After this, there aremlines describing the streets. Each line has two integersaandb: there is a
one-way street from crossingato crossingb. There is at most one street between any two crossings in a given
direction, and there is no street directly from crossing 1 to crossingn.
Output Format
Print one integerk: the minimum number of crossings that must be shut down so that no route from crossing 1
to crossingnremains.
