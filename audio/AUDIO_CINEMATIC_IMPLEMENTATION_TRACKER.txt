CODE OWNERS
-----------

core/src/main/java/com/infectedhour/core/content/DialogueCatalog.java
  Exact image, subtitle, speaker, title, and OGG mapping for every scene.

core/src/main/java/com/infectedhour/core/screens/StoryPanelScreen.java
  Loads images, renders subtitles, plays/stops narration, handles continue/skip,
  synchronizes co-op advancement, and routes to the next level.

core/src/main/java/com/infectedhour/core/screens/GameScreen.java
  Sends completed Maps 1-5 into their next cinematic sequence.

core/src/main/java/com/infectedhour/core/screens/BossScreen.java
  Sends final victory into the two ending cinematics.

core/src/main/java/com/infectedhour/core/InfectedHourGame.java
  Routes developer split-screen map selections through their matching opening
  cinematic; normal LAN co-op uses the same StoryPanelScreen event flow.

core/src/main/java/com/infectedhour/core/audio/AudioDirector.java
  Safely plays optional OGG narration. Missing files never crash the game.


CONTROLS
--------

E / Space / Enter / Left Click: reveal subtitle, then continue
S: skip the current cinematic sequence
