🎮 The Infected Hour — Online Co-op Presentation Guide

Context: Our final project evaluation will take place online via video call (Google Meet / Zoom / Discord). Below are the 3 tested methods to present the multiplayer/co-op gameplay smoothly.

Option 1: Split-Screen Side-by-Side Demo on 1 PC ⭐ STRONGLY RECOMMENDED

Why this is best: 100% reliable, zero Wi-Fi drop risk, and gives the teacher simultaneous live proof of synchronization.

Why Teachers Prefer This:

  • No waiting for screen switches or handling multi-student stream lag.
  • Teacher sees both players simultaneously on a single shared screen.
  • When Player 1 attacks/moves on the left, Player 2's screen on the right updates instantly (proving 20Hz UDP snapshot replication & TCP event delivery).

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Terminal 1 — Start the Backend: 
    .\gradlew.bat :backend:bootRun
  2. Terminal 2 — Start Player 1 (Host / Elric): 
    .\gradlew.bat :lwjgl3:run
  3. Terminal 3 — Start Player 2 (Client / Jane): 
    .\gradlew.bat :lwjgl3:run --args="join"
    (Automatically connects to localhost 127.0.0.1!)
  4. Arrange Windows:
    • Press F11 on both windows to switch to Windowed mode (1280×720).
    • Snap Player 1 to the Left half of screen: Win + ←
    • Snap Player 2 to the Right half of screen: Win + →
  5. Presenting: Share your entire screen on Google Meet / Zoom. Alternate movement to show synchronized co-op.

Option 2: Virtual LAN via Radmin VPN FOR SEPARATE HOUSES

Use this when: Two teammates are in different locations and both want to play live using their own keyboards/mice.

Setup (Takes ~3 minutes):

  1. Both teammates install free Radmin VPN (no account required).
  2. Host Teammate:
    • Clicks "Create Network" → Sets Name (e.g. InfectedHourDemo) and password.
    • Starts backend: .\gradlew.bat :backend:bootRun
    • Starts Host launcher: .\gradlew.bat :fx-launcher:run → Host Game.
    • Notes down their Radmin IP (e.g., 26.152.88.42).
  3. Client Teammate (at home):
    • Clicks "Join Network" → Enters the same name & password.
    • Starts launcher: .\gradlew.bat :fx-launcher:run
    • Goes to Co-op Mode → Join a Match.
    • In the "…or connect directly by IP" box, types the Host's Radmin IP (e.g. 26.152.88.42).
    • Clicks Join.
  4. Presenting: Host shares screen on Meet/Zoom while both teammates play and voice chat.

Option 3: Pre-recorded Video Clip SAFETY NET (MUST PREPARE)

Why: Live internet streams on Google Meet can occasionally lag or stutter. Having a clean 60–90 second clip prepared removes 100% of presentation anxiety.

What to Record:

  • Lobby setup / Character selection (Elric & Jane).
  • Both players coordinating movement and combat.
  • Objective sync and player revive mechanic.

Script If Needed:

"Sir, we have the live system running, and we also have a high-definition 60-second capture demonstrating the 2-player packet sync in case Meet's screen-share bandwidth stutters."
